## 👑 Game Overview & Lore
**KALB** *(Konsortium of Administration, Law and Business)* is an international organisation with a single goal: **make profit whatever the cost**. Its members are CEOs, politicians, scientists, artists, and journalists who share one distinct trait: absolute evilness. 

When their president, Wanda Olsam, goes missing, they do the only thing imaginable—they fight for the throne! As an esteemed member of KALB, will you be the first to reach the leader's seat in a world of fragile alliances, sketchy plots, and permanent danger?

## 🛠️ Game Setup (Step-by-Step)

1. **Procure Provisions:** Before starting, ensure you have four bottles of different flavors, ranging between 20-30% alcohol content.
2. **Deploy Canvas:** Place the main game board in the dead center of the table.
3. **Distribute Ladders:** Place one physical ladder token in each of the four corners of the board.
4. **Establish the Lineup:** On one side of the board, arrange the alcohol bottles in a straight line. Place **The Crown** directly on top of the first bottle.
5. **Select Roles:** Each player picks a character class using your favorite selection method. 
6. **Distribute Assets:** Players receive their respective Class Card, matching pawn, and any unique tokens needed for their role.
7. **Position Pawns:** Every player places their pawn on **Room 0 (START)**.
8. **Appoint the Opener:** Everyone votes for the player who will take the first turn.

## 🎯 The Object of the Game

> ### 🏁 The Win Condition
> A player wins immediately upon landing **exactly** on **Room 60**. 
>
> If you roll a number higher than the distance required to reach Room 60, your pawn **bounces backward** off the wall.
>
> *Example:* If you are currently standing on Room 57 and roll a `5`, you will move forward 3 steps to Room 60, then bounce backward 2 steps, ultimately landing on Room 58.

## 🔄 How to Play (The Core Loop)

### Structure of a Round
Gameplay proceeds clockwise, player by player. 

::: warning-box
⚠️ **State Change Interaction:** Whenever a player changes rooms *outside* of their own turn (due to displacement effects or abilities), they **do not trigger** the room's text effect.
:::

*Pay close attention to cooldowns and ability refreshes! Certain actions or passives (such as the Priest's passive) can occur during an opponent's turn, but can only be executed once per round.*

### Anatomy of a Turn
On your turn, you **must** complete your Mandatory Movement and **may** choose to use your active ability. You possess complete tactical flexibility over your sequence. Choose either **Option A** or **Option B**:

*   **Option A:** Execute Movement → Resolve the room/ladder landed on → Trigger Active Ability.
*   **Option B:** Trigger Active Ability → Execute Movement → Resolve the room/ladder landed on.

*Note: You cannot activate abilities mid-movement or midway through a ladder event. You must explicitly announce to the group when your turn is officially over.*

## 🧪 The Drinking Rules & The Crown

*   **What is "Š"?** `Š` stands explicitly for **Shots**.
*   **The Rotating Crown:** The Crown always sits atop one of the bottles in the line. Whenever a board rule, card ability, or event forces a player to take a shot, that shot **must** be poured exclusively from the crowned bottle.
*   **Moving the Crown:** Immediately after the shot instance is completely resolved and consumed, the physical Crown shifts to the next bottle in the line sequence.

## 🪜 Detailed Rules & Key Concepts

### Resolving Room Effects
When your movement lands you in a room, resolve that room's printed effect (drinking, mini-game, teleport, etc.) before doing anything else. If that effect sends you somewhere else, resolve the new room's effect too, and so on, until you land somewhere with no further displacement.

The one exception is a ladder: if the room you land in contains a ladder, you do not resolve its printed text directly. Instead, resolve the Ladder Event first — then resolve the effect of whichever room you end up in once the ladder event is settled.

### Moving Ladders
Whenever your movement die shows a **6**, you may move one of the four ladders at the end of your turn, reconnecting it between any two rooms you choose (Room 60 excepted — see FAQ). Classes that roll multiple dice for movement can show multiple 6s in the same turn, and may move one ladder per 6 shown.

### Ladder Events
When a movement route intersects with a ladder:

1. The active player declares whether they intend to climb the ladder (if moving upward) or avoid it (if moving downward).
2. **The Challenge:** Any opponent can instantly challenge the active player's choice by drinking **1 Š**.
3. **The Bidding War:** Players may alternate bidding, with each counter-challenge requiring **+1 Š higher** than the last bid, until all opponents yield or the active player gives up.

Only the active player can ever trigger a Ladder Event, no matter what caused them to enter the room — but it always requires movement to have happened first that turn; a player simply sitting in a laddered room does not re-trigger it at the start of their next turn.

If resolving a Ladder Event lands you in a room where a different ladder also has an end, you immediately resolve a new Ladder Event for it — ladders can chain this way. The same ladder, however, cannot be used twice in the same turn.

### Bound
Some cards and rooms make two players **bound** to each other. Whenever a bound player has to drink for any reason other than a shot (**Š**), the player they are bound to must drink the same amount. A player can only be bound to one other player at a time — entering a new bond immediately breaks their previous one.

### Mini-Games & Keywords

*   **RPS:** A classic Rock-Paper-Scissors game.
*   **Collective RPS:** All players throw Rock-Paper-Scissors at once. Each player drinks one sip for every opponent whose sign beats theirs.
*   **Royal RPS:** The active player plays a separate round of RPS against each other player in turn, one at a time.
*   **Suitcase:** The first player starts with "In my suitcase, I have..." followed by an item or expression. Each following player repeats the entire list in order, then adds one new item. Whoever fails to repeat the full sequence correctly drinks one sip. *(Optional harder variant: they drink a number of sips equal to the length of the sequence instead.)*
*   **X or Y:** The active player poses a dilemma between two options. Every other player votes with a thumbs up (option X) or thumbs down (option Y); whoever picked the less-popular option drinks.
*   **According to...:** The active player names another player ("According to [Name], who...") and asks a question whose answer is a player at the table. Everyone else guesses who [Name] is thinking of and points at their guess at the same time; [Name] then reveals who they really meant, and anyone who guessed wrong drinks one sip.
*   **Is this anecdote true or false?:** The active player tells an anecdote about another player at the table. Everyone else guesses whether it's true or false; anyone who guesses wrong drinks one sip.
*   **Never have I ever:** Standard rules.
*   **Would you rather:** Standard rules.
*   **Queen of Questions:** A title granted by landing on Room 24. While you hold it, you may give a sip to anyone who answers a question you ask.
*   **little man in your glass:** While active, you must take the (imaginary) little man out of your glass and set him on the table before drinking, then put him back in afterward — every single time you drink.

## 📑 Quick Reference & FAQ

### Turn Cheat-Sheet
1. **Assess State:** Check class card cooldowns.
2. **Act or Move:** Execute Option A or Option B.
3. **Resolve:** Process Room text or Ladder triggers.
4. **Pass:** Verbally announce the end of your turn.

### Frequently Asked Questions
* **Q: Can I use a ladder shortcut to enter Room 60?**
  * *A:* Absolutely not. Room 60 is strictly **Impenetrable**; no ladder can be anchored to it or bypass its borders.
* **Q: Do I match a shot if the player I'm Bound to takes one?**
  * *A:* No. The Bound dynamic applies strictly to standard sips. `Š` actions are individual unless explicitly stated otherwise.
* **Q: What if I get moved onto Room 60 by another player's ability, but it's not my turn?**
  * *A:* You do not win — only the active player triggers room effects (see the State Change Interaction rule), and the win condition is Room 60's effect. You simply remain there. On your next turn, roll normally: since you're already on Room 60, any roll overshoots by its full value, and you bounce straight back that many rooms.
