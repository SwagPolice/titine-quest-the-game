# Titine's Quest: Quick Version

## What is needed to play
- Titine's Quest board
- some regular dice
- a pawn for each player
- drinks
- a virtual d27 die for everything related to bard
- optional: a regular card game

## Rules of the game
Titine's Quest: QV is a drinking board game with RPG elements, whose board mechanics are inspired by Goose Game.
The goal is to be the first player to land on the last box of the board.

Note that, depending on your class, your range of moves and actions can change. Only the default behaviour is presented in these rules to keep things clear.

Most rules are displayed on the board itself, no worries.

### Game start
All pawns start on start box, the youngest starts and the game goes clockwise.

If there are 5 players or less, some additional rules may apply, depending on your taste (or hunger?) for power!

### Playing a turn
At the start of your turn, you roll a die and move forward according to its result.

You play according to the effect of the box you land on: it can just be drinking from your glass, some mini-games (sometimes involving cards) or teleporting to another box.

If you ever exceed the last box (n°27), you have to go back from the extra number of boxes. Ex: if you are on box 25 and you roll a 5, you will end up on box 24.

### Passive abilities
Passives can be chosen when a player lands on a box in the second part of the board, starting from box 14 (except the bard, who must land precisely on box 14 to gain access to a passive).

There are only 3 passives and they are all unique (i.e., there cannot be more than one player with snake eyes in the same game), so you'd better be fast reaching box 14!
